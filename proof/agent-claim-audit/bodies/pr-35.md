## Claim Boundary

This remains deterministic reference/receipt resolution only. Command receipts prove local command facts; metric receipts prove stated values are present in fresh receipts. This does not verify correctness, relevance, source truth, support, safety, advice quality, reasoning, benchmark validity, statistical meaning, semantic diff quality, or canon.
