## Summary

Covers CORE-955 and CORE-958.

Adds `anti-slop-claims` as the generic Markdown/text artifact-checking entrypoint while preserving `anti-slop-pr`, `anti-slop-pr-event`, and `anti-slop-lineage` behavior.

This is a packaging/API shape change first, not a semantic expansion. The claim boundary remains reference/receipt resolution only.
