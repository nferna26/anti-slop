# Source Card

## Claim

A specimen record is only as trustworthy as its locality locator; a record
naming a finer locality than was actually determined over-claims precision the
collection cannot support.

## Evidence locator

Chapter 5, Section 3.

## Short excerpt

None.

## Paraphrase

State locality only as precisely as it was actually determined; do not upgrade a
coarse "northern range" record into a precise grid reference.

## Scope conditions

Applies to specimen records carrying a stated collection locality. The carded
claim is the precision-discipline rule, not any specific locality's correctness.

## Misuse risk

Upgrading a coarse locality into a precise grid reference the card never carried,
then citing the upgraded precision as if the card supported it.
