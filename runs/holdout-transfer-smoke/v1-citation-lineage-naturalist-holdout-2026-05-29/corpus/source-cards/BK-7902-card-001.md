# Source Card

## Claim

A change in repeat plant-count totals across visits is not evidence of
population change until the seasonal visibility confound (some plants are only
countable while in flower) has been separated out.

## Evidence locator

Chapter 3.

## Short excerpt

None.

## Paraphrase

Control the in-flower vs out-of-flower visibility confound before reading a
count change as a population change.

## Scope conditions

Applies to repeat transect counts of plants with seasonal visibility. Does not,
on its own, decide whether any observed change is real — only that the seasonal
confound must be controlled first.

## Misuse risk

Reporting a drop between an in-flower and an out-of-flower visit as a population
decline, citing this card as support for the decline rather than for the
confound warning it actually carries.
