# Source Card

## Claim

A logged field observation records what an observer actually saw a specimen do;
attributing a cause or motive to that behaviour is a separate inference the log
entry must not silently fold into the observation.

## Evidence locator

Chapter 2.

## Short excerpt

None.

## Paraphrase

Keep observation and inference in distinct log layers: "returned to the same
branch" is an observation; "was defending territory" is an inference that needs
its own support.

## Scope conditions

Applies to direct-observation field notes where behaviour is recorded before any
causal explanation. Does not establish that any particular inferred cause is
wrong — only that observation and inference are distinct layers.

## Misuse risk

Citing a behaviour log as if it proved the inferred motive.
