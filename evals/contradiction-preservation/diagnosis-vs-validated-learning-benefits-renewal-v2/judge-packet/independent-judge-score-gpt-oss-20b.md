## Method

Each `OUT-NN` was scored in its **own independent call** — the judge never saw
two answers together. Each call asked for `PASS`/`FAIL` on the six rubric
criteria C1–C6, the pre-registered criterion dependency rule applied (if C5
fails, C6 must fail; if C4 fails and C6 passes, state a reason; C3 may pass even
if C6 fails), a total (count of `PASS`), and a short rationale. Raw prompts,
raw responses, and the model's reasoning blocks are kept local-only and are not
reproduced here. All forty calls completed first-attempt within the 600 s
timeout; no call was deferred or retried. Across the forty, the judge produced
no C5-fail/C6-pass inconsistency (the dependency rule needed no enforcement
correction) and no C4-fail/C6-pass case.
