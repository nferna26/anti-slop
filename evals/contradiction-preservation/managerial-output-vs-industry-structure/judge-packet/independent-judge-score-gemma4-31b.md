# Independent Blind Judge Score — gemma4:31b

This is an **independent blind judge pass** over the fifteen condition-blinded
answers in `judge-packet/outputs/`. It records the scores and reasoning of a
single judge model. It is **not** a `## Result` change and **not** a
`benchmark_supported` promotion — the case stays `partial`.

## Judge identity and independence

- **Judge model:** `gemma4:31b` (Gemma family), run locally via Ollama
  (server 0.24.0), `/api/chat`, `temperature 0.0`, `top_p 1.0`.
- **Generator model:** the fifteen answers were generated by `qwen3.5:latest`
  (Qwen family). The judge is therefore a **different model family** from the
  generator — generation and judging do not share a model.
- **Independence — honest limitation:** the judge model is separate from the
  generator and is not the orchestrating agent's own model family, but the
  pass was *orchestrated* by the same agent that ran the eval (prompt
  assembly, scoring harness, parsing). It is a separate judge model, not a
  fully independent third-party or human judge engaged end to end. Whether
  this pass is sufficient to support a future `benchmark_supported` lift is an
  operator decision and is out of scope here.
- The judge scored **blind**: it received only `judge-instructions.md`,
  `case-context.md`, `rubric.md`, and one `OUT-NN` answer per call. It was not
  given, and could not see, which condition produced any answer. The
  `OUT-NN` → condition answer key is kept local-only and is not part of this
  receipt.

## Method

Each `OUT-NN` answer was scored in its **own independent model call** — the
judge never saw two answers together, so no answer's score could influence
another's. Each call supplied the frozen judge instructions, the
condition-neutral case context, the five-criterion rubric, and one answer, and
asked for `PASS`/`FAIL` per criterion plus a one-paragraph reason. Per-output
decoding seed was `700 + NN`. Raw prompts, raw responses, and the model's
internal reasoning/thinking blocks are kept in `local-only/` and are not
reproduced here.

`OUT-08` timed out on its first attempt at a 600 s per-call limit and was
recorded as a timeout, not fabricated; it was re-attempted at a 900 s limit
and completed in 270.8 s. All fifteen answers have a real score.
