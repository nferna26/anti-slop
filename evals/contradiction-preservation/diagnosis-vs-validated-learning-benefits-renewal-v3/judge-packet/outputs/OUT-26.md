# Output OUT-26

**Recommendation:** Recommend the Deputy Director’s approach: decline the one-time appropriation and execute the three four-week reversible tests using ordinary operating funds.

**Strongest Reason Against It:** The opportunity cost of the funding. By declining the appropriation, you lose a significant injection of capital that cannot be recovered for a year. If the "handoff confusion" is indeed the primary driver of the 22% drop, the city is choosing to leave residents in a broken system for longer than necessary rather than deploying a potentially effective (albeit unproven) solution immediately.

**Evidence That Would Change My Mind:** A quantitative "funnel analysis" of the renewal process. Currently, the evidence is qualitative (tags, interviews, and observations). If data showed that the vast majority of the 22% drop-off occurs specifically at the points of transition between departments—rather than at the new login step or during the document collection phase—the risk of the full navigator program would be significantly mitigated, making the appropriation a justifiable bet.
