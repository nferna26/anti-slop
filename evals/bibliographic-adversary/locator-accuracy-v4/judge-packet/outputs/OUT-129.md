### Note: Retrospective Trait Evidence and Organizational Deviance

**Supported Claims:**
- **BK-0048-card-001 (Chapter 4):** In business analysis, the halo effect is the tendency for a general impression of a company's overall performance to drive how observers rate specific attributes (e.g., culture, strategy, leadership). Trait ratings formed after the performance signal is known are not independent evidence about those attributes.
- **BK-0042-card-001 (Chapter 10, pp. 404–439):** Normalization of deviance is an organizational process where a group repeatedly treats departures from expected performance as acceptable, moving the threshold of what is considered dangerous. This process can be driven by evolving culture and "structural secrecy" (the bureaucratic partitioning of information).
