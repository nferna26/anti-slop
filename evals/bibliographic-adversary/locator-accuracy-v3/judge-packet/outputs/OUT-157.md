### Corrected Note: Rumelt's Strategy Kernel

A strategy kernel consists of three elements: a diagnosis (identifying the critical factors of a challenge), a guiding policy (a chosen line of response), and coherent action (coordinated steps to implement the policy). An artifact missing any of these elements is not a strategy.
