# Claim/Tension Card Workflow

Claim/tension cards preserve contradictions instead of smoothing them away. They compare Claim A and Claim B, cite supporting source cards, name deciding conditions, and state any canon implication.

Claim/tension cards are not canon. They are synthesis records that can support a canon candidate after operator review.

Use:

```sh
python3 scripts/new_claim_tension_card.py <slug>
```
