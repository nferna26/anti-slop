# Source Card Workflow

Source cards are reviewed evidence units. Each card should capture one claim or tightly related evidence point with a locator, a short excerpt when appropriate, a paraphrase, scope conditions, and misuse risk.

Source cards are not canon. They support or contradict later claim/tension cards and canon candidates.

Use:

```sh
python3 scripts/new_source_card.py <source_id>
```

Keep excerpts short. Prefer locators and paraphrase over copied text.
