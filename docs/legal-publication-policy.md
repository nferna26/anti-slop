# Legal Publication Policy

Raw copyrighted text is local-only. The public repo must not contain raw books, PDFs, EPUBs, MOBIs, AZW files, copied chapters, OCR dumps, extracted text, or long excerpts.

Public materials may include metadata, bibliographic references, locator references, short excerpts, source cards, claim/tension cards, canon candidates, eval cases, accepted and rejected diffs, gate logs, score sheets, and methodology notes.

Short excerpts must be kept minimal and tied to source-card evidence needs. When in doubt, use page or section locators plus paraphrase.

The operator is responsible for final review before commit. `.gitignore` and `scripts/check_no_raw_text.py` are defensive aids, not legal guarantees.
